When to vote

City of Crossville municipal election is Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026. Early voting is Oct. 14 through Oct. 29. Cumberland County Commission, County Mayor, and Sheriff were on the Aug. 6 county general. Those races are over. The people who won still owe a public ALPR answer.

Aug. 6 already happened

Commission winners come from the Aug. 6 Election Summary Report archived Aug. 26, 2026. They cannot cancel Crossville’s city cameras. They can still ban Flock, Axon, and other county ALPRs. Roster and meetings: Cumberland page.

Ask in writing

The same question for everyone

Ask at forums, on campaign pages, at the door, and in letters the paper will print. Sitting officials with a published inbox can be emailed. Challengers on the 3B list did not publish campaign emails there. Do not use a city form as if they already hold the seat.

City Council candidates

Cancel and remove. No vendor swap. If they will not cancel, a recorded yearly vote plus a map and monthly search reports.

Will you vote to cancel Crossville’s Flock contract, remove the cameras, and refuse any plate-reader replacement (Flock, Axon, or otherwise)? If you will not cancel, will you require a recorded public yes-or-no vote each year before any renewal, plus a public camera map and monthly search reports (who searched, what, why, hits, and sharing)? Please put that answer on the record.

County Mayor, Sheriff, Commission

This site has not found a county Flock or ALPR contract. Ask them to ban those tools.

Will you vote to ban county purchase, lease, funding, or operation of Flock, Axon, or any other automated license-plate reader or similar AI mass-surveillance tool? Confirm in writing whether the county or Sheriff already has any such agreement. Please put that answer on the record.

Send a quoted answer to info@crossvilleprivacy.org with a source. We will add it here. Silence stays on the table as “No public answer.”
