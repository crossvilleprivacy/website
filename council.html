    What can I do

    Say these asks at council, and put them in email too.

    1. Ask for this first: Cancel Crossville’s Flock contract and remove the cameras.
    2. If they will not cancel yet: Ask for a public hearing announced ahead of time and a recorded yes-or-no vote each year before any renewal or expansion, with no automatic renewal that skips a public vote, plus a public camera map, written search rules, and monthly detailed reports the public can check (who searched, what they searched, why, hits, and sharing). If the program cannot win that yearly vote, it should not stay.
    3. Transparency (not a substitute for removal): Written search policy, supervisor approval before plate lookups, public camera map, and a list of who can share or query the data.
    4. How you can help: Come to the next work session or council meeting, bring a neighbor, pick a 3-minute speech script (or the generic outline), and email city leaders with the same ask. Only City Council can put cancel on the agenda. There is no remaining meeting before this renewal, so keep filling the room until they vote to cancel and remove the cameras.

    Suggested copy-paste line:

    I support public safety and privacy. Please cancel Crossville’s Flock contract and remove the cameras. If you will not cancel yet, please announce a public hearing ahead of time and take a recorded City Council vote each year before any renewal, with no automatic renewal that skips a public vote, and publish monthly detailed Flock-use reports the public can check (who searched, what they searched, why, hits, and sharing), plus the policy on how data is used, supervisor-approval rules, and a public camera map. I will be at the next council meeting.

    Other questions worth asking

    There is no remaining City Council meeting before this renewal. These questions still belong on the record. The live ask is cancel the contract and remove the cameras.

    • Who can search Crossville plate reads, for what reasons, and with what supervisor approval?
    • Which outside agencies, including federal, can see or query Crossville’s data, and what written guarantees exist after Flock changed its story on federal access?
    • Can police outside Cumberland County search our plates? Is Crossville enrolled in Flock’s National Lookup nationwide sharing program, and which out-of-state or federal agencies can request local reads?
    • Is FreeForm enabled (type a description, including clothing or people, and search camera footage)? Who can run it, and how is it audited?
    • Does Crossville have Multi-Geo and/or Convoy Search (tools that track a plate across cities or flag cars that travel together)? Who can run them, with what supervisor approval, and will the city publish use counts?
    • Has council compared Flock’s public “no facial recognition / no race-gender” marketing with what its patent claims about searching people by appearance, and will the city publish which features are enabled?
    • Does Crossville’s live Flock contract include a vendor “upgrades to platform” clause (other cities’ public agreements let Flock push upgrades in its sole discretion)? Who is notified if people-search or demographic features change?
    • Are Investigations Manager / Multi-State Insights or other AI “suspicious activity” pattern tools enabled (ACLU analysis archived Aug. 13, 2026), and how do they relate to Multi-Geo / Convoy?
    • Has Crossville received any notice of the Dec. 2025 open Condor (Flock people-tracking video camera) livestream exposure, the 51 cybersecurity findings / related CVEs, or overseas annotation of U.S. imagery, and what models/security terms are in the live contract?
    • Will Crossville publish retention settings under TCA § 55-10-302 archived May 5, 2025? A map and monthly reports help, but they are not a substitute for removing the cameras.
