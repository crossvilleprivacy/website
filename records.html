TPRA audit log

Crossville PD Flock lookups

This is a volume and pattern audit of Crossville PD Flock use, July 2023 through August 2026, from the audit logs the city released under the Tennessee Public Records Act. Search by tokenized officer ID, date, row ID, or SHA-256 hash. Five of 37 officers ran half of the 10,645 lookups; Officer-21 ran about ten times as many as a typical officer. That is a volume pattern, not a charge of misuse. Reasons are the original officer-typed text, including their spelling. Case numbers, plates, and other identifiers are replaced with [redacted]. Ordinary-crime reasons such as theft, warrants, and narcotics are still in the table. We collapsed the most sensitive officer-typed case labels so this page cannot be used as a victim or investigation index. Those rows are marked [redacted] and show month and year only, not the day.

Officer names are tokenized, such as Officer-1. CrossvillePrivacy.org did those redactions. The city and the department did not. The files they produced under TPRA still had those identifiers in them. We took those fields out, including officer names, so this page can show how the cameras are used without becoming a public roster of officers or a list of who got searched. Tokenized IDs still show the volume pattern. Real names in a 10,000-row public table would not.

Each public row has a number (1–10,645) and a SHA-256 hash of the original unredacted row. Rows 1–10,415 follow the city’s first PDF order; rows 10,416–10,645 append the July 28–August 31, 2026 follow-up production. Case # is Yes, No, or Unknown. Unknown means the city’s export did not make it clear whether a case number was present.
